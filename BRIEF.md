# Project brief: PlanBee

## The competition
- I’m entering the Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), a national K–12 competition. My age group: Middle school (grades 6–8)
- Judges score only a written entry (a PDF of up to 10 pages) and a video of up to 4 minutes. They reward a clear understanding of the problem and a sensible use of AI.
- My deadlines: The registration deadline is December 18 at 5:00pm. The project submission deadline is December 23 at 5:00 pm. For the project submission you have to type up a 10 page written entry and make a video showcasing the website.

## The problem
- People in my class struggle with their sleep schedules because homework keeps them up late, and doomscrolling on their phones keeps them up later.
- Impact area: Health and wellbeing
- Why it matters (impact): Young teenagers can go to bed earlier at night and be less stressed out. Homework can't really be avoided, but good time management could help.
- Evidence it’s real: Only about one in every four students get the full recommended eight hours of sleep every night. From the 2010s to the 2020s, the percentage of students with a full eight-hour sleep schedule decreased. (https://www.cdc.gov/yrbs/dstr/dietary-physical-sleep-behaviors.html).
- What people told me:
  - Person A: "I literally never get eight hours of sleep, mostly because of homework. Although I can't be on my phone at night because of my screen time limit, I would be if I didn't have one. I just listen to music, and normally I get to bed from midnight to one in the morning."
  - Person B: "I get home around 4 on most days. I have many extracurriculars and I only start homework for thirty minutes once I come home at 11. I usually get to bed around 2:30am. I have to catch up my sleep in any free time I have, such as on the car."

## My solution
- My AI’s job: My AI helps by sorting assignments by importance, calculating due dates, and creating schedules and planners instantly to help improve sleep schedules, which works better than having to write everything by hand since someone might miss something or forget to write something.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: There are many online planners out there, such as MyStudyLife or even Google Calendar, but a lot of them require a paid subscription and don't cater student-by-student. I want my website to work for any student, have quick time management tips, and be honest when to suggest to maybe ask for an extension.
- Safety plan: People might type in their own personal information, so I'll make sure to include a note that says to not type any personal info. Tasks might not be ordered by due date, so I'll make sure that people can type in a date for each one and the planner is organized by date.

## My AI’s instructions
- When it answers: There's a pop-up that asks the users to type in their daily tasks and their due dates, and the AI puts them in order of importance and urgency so students know what to prioritize. The user presses a button that says 'Add', and it is added to the planner.
- Its job: The AI's job is to order tasks using importance and urgency, and remind the students to take a break, or ask for an extension when too many tasks are piled on.
- Who it talks to and how to sound: The AI is talking to students, most likely late elementary up to high schoolers, and it should be cheerful but not annoying. It shouldn't make jokes or go off topic, and it shouldn't encourage procrastination. The AI should be polite and helpful.
- Facts it must use: The facts that the AI should use are how students shouldn't sleep too late, probably at most 12:00am.
- Rules it must follow: The AI must never encourage pulling all-nighters, pile a bunch of tasks into one day, go off topic, or encourage procrastination. The AI is especially not allowed to ever do students' homework for them. Breaks are allowed, but not procrastination.
- How to answer: The answers aren't too long, since the AI is mostly used for planning. The AI should always just get to the point, and not waste time talking about unrelated things.
